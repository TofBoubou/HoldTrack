DanceTrack annotations

holdtrack/identity/data/second_link_pairs.npz holds features and labels derived from the annotations of the DanceTrack training and validation videos.
DanceTrack annotations are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0): https://github.com/DanceTrack/DanceTrack
Sun et al., "DanceTrack: Multi-Object Tracking in Uniform Appearance and Diverse Motion", 2022.
